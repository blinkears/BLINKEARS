ABOUT BLINKEARS

A little glow can lead to a very big adventure.

BLINKEARS is an original animated world built around Siko & Rumba — two unforgettable characters whose curiosity turns everyday moments into magical adventures.

Warm, funny and cinematic, BLINKEARS is being developed as a growing world of stories, characters and experiences.
